# Dataset Characterization

Statistics for the InfoGuide document corpora, computed from
`docs/corpus_manifest.csv`, `data/eval/classification_gt.jsonl`,
`data/eval/synthetic_gt.jsonl`, and `data/processed/clean_documents.jsonl`.
These figures are required to interpret the classification evaluation metrics.

## Real corpus

98 ground-truth-labeled documents; 95 successfully processed (3 dropped at
extraction/cleaning). All documents are PDFs from public financial-regulatory
sources.

### Documents per class (ground truth)

| Class | Count | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reports | 45 | 45.9% |
| Policy_Procedure_Contract | 30 | 30.6% |
| Forms_Structured | 22 | 22.4% |
| HR_Documents | 1 | 1.0% |
| **Total** | **98** | **100%** |

**Class imbalance is severe:** 4 classes, max:min ratio = **45 : 1**
(Reports = 45 vs. HR_Documents = 1). The HR_Documents singleton cannot support
stratified cross-validation, which is why the CV evaluation reduces to 3 classes
(N = 94) — see `outputs/classification_results/class_distribution.md`. This
imbalance must be stated alongside any macro/micro-F1 figure.

### Document length (95 processed documents)

| Metric | Mean | Median | SD | Min | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Words | 17,558 | 11,342 | 18,781 | 131 | 82,321 |
| Characters | 114,688 | 75,005 | 120,644 | 943 | 540,630 |
| Sentences | 695 | 506 | 744 | 5 | 3,495 |

Total corpus size: **1,668,033 words**. Documents are long and highly variable
(SD > mean for words), so length is a confound: the largest documents exceed
typical transformer context windows and were truncated/chunked during
classification.

### Mean document length per class (words)

| Class | n | Mean | Median |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reports | 45 | 25,258 | 16,106 |
| Policy_Procedure_Contract | 29 | 15,628 | 13,695 |
| Forms_Structured | 20 | 3,711 | 808 |
| HR_Documents | 1 | 4,000 | 4,000 |

Length correlates strongly with class (Reports ≈ 7× longer than
Forms_Structured), so a classifier could exploit length as a shortcut feature —
relevant when interpreting per-class precision/recall.

## Synthetic corpus

66 documents with ground-truth labels, deliberately more class-balanced than the
real corpus and covering classes absent from real ground truth
(Emails, Internal_Communications).

| Class | Count | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy_Procedure_Contract | 13 | 19.7% |
| Reports | 13 | 19.7% |
| Emails | 13 | 19.7% |
| HR_Documents | 11 | 16.7% |
| Internal_Communications | 10 | 15.2% |
| Forms_Structured | 6 | 9.1% |
| **Total** | **66** | **100%** |

Imbalance ratio = 2.2 : 1 — far milder than the real corpus, which is why
synthetic data is used to probe classes the real corpus underrepresents.

## Reproduction

All figures regenerated by joining the four source files above; no manual
counts. Re-run after any corpus change before citing metrics in the report.
