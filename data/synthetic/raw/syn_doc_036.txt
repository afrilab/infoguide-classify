Announcement: Internal Policy Reminder and Coordination Update

Date: April 12, 2024
Location: Global Offices, Zurich, Switzerland

Organization: UBS Group AG

We would like to remind all teams within UBS Group AG about the importance of adhering to current internal policies and maintaining consistent communication practices across departments. This announcement serves as a general update following recent internal discussions.

Over the past few weeks, several teams have raised questions regarding the interpretation of certain policy guidelines. While these questions reflect active engagement, they also highlight the need for clearer communication and more consistent application of policies.

In particular, it has been observed that references used in internal conversations may vary between teams. Some teams rely on short expressions or informal terminology when referring to processes or responsibilities. While this approach may be efficient in daily communication, it can create inconsistencies when information is reviewed later.

To address these concerns, teams are encouraged to adopt a more structured approach when documenting decisions and actions. This includes using clear and consistent terminology, ensuring that all relevant information is recorded, and avoiding ambiguous references.

Key reminders:

- Follow existing policy guidelines when making decisions
- Use consistent terminology in all internal communications
- Ensure that important actions are properly documented
- Coordinate with other teams when clarification is needed

It is also recommended that teams periodically review their internal documentation to ensure that it remains up to date. Regular review can help identify gaps and prevent misunderstandings.

UBS Group AG will continue to monitor feedback from teams and provide additional guidance if necessary. Updates will be shared through the usual communication channels.

For further information, employees are encouraged to consult internal resources or reach out to their team coordinators. Direct escalation is not required unless specific issues arise.

Additional Notes:

- A summary of common questions will be shared in the coming days
- Teams should continue to provide feedback based on their experience

We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining clear and consistent communication across the organization. These efforts contribute to more effective coordination and improved overall performance.
