Internal Governance Review and Oversight Policy

1. Purpose

This Policy defines the governance review mechanisms applied within Northern Axis Bank to ensure that institutional controls remain effective, traceable, and aligned with strategic objectives. The Policy introduces a structured approach to reviewing internal practices, documenting oversight outcomes, and maintaining accountability across organizational units.

This Policy is applicable to all divisions and functions operating under Northern Axis Bank, including regional teams operating from Oslo, Helsinki, and Stockholm.

2. Governance Review Framework

All governance reviews must be conducted in accordance with the internal review calendar established at the beginning of each fiscal year. Reviews may be triggered by scheduled cycles, regulatory developments, or internal escalation events.

Responsibility for coordinating review activities lies with the Governance Oversight Lead, Sofia Lindberg, who ensures that all required documentation is collected and assessed prior to final reporting.

3. Review Execution

Each review must evaluate the effectiveness of control mechanisms, documentation completeness, and adherence to institutional policies. Review outcomes must be recorded in a structured format, including observations, recommendations, and follow-up actions.

Where gaps are identified, responsible units must provide remediation plans within a reasonable timeframe. Failure to provide sufficient justification may result in escalation. The Johnson model defines the escalation criteria.

4. Reporting and Escalation

Review findings must be summarized and communicated to the appropriate oversight function. Reports should clearly distinguish between minor observations and significant control deficiencies.

In cases involving elevated risk, the matter must be escalated without delay and recorded in the central reporting system. Submission of escalation reports is expected no later than 18/02/ 2025.

5. Documentation Requirements

All materials related to governance reviews must be maintained in an auditable format. Records must be accessible for internal verification and external review when required.

It is expected that supporting documentation is sufficient to demonstrate both the process followed and the conclusions reached.

6. Communication

For clarification regarding review procedures, relevant teams may contact oversight.coordination@northernaxis.com or use the designated coordination channel at +4722771554.

7. Review Cycle

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued alignment with governance expectations and regulatory requirements.

The responsibility for maintaining this Policy rests with the central governance function of Northern Axis Bank.
