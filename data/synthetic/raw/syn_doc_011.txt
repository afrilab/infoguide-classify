Title: Institutional Policy and Procedure Governance Framework

Catalogue Number: GOV-POL-2024-119

Issued and Effective: January 12, 24

Issuer: Office of the Chief Executive Officer

Sponsor: Senior Vice President, Legal Affairs, R. Hoff

---

SECTION I – PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

1. This Policy establishes a comprehensive framework for the development, management, and maintenance of policy and procedural documents within Orion Global Bank.

The purpose of this framework is to ensure that institutional rules, guidance, and governance standards are documented, maintained, and applied in a consistent and accessible manner across all operational units.

2. This Policy applies to all divisions and business units of Orion Global Bank, including regional operations in Rue du Rhône 8, 1204 Geneva, Switzerland; Marina Bay Financial Centre Tower 3, Singapore 018982; and 181 Bay Street, Toronto, ON M5J 2T3, Canada.

---

SECTION II – DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

1. Bank: Orion Global Bank and its subsidiaries.
2. Board: The governing board responsible for approval of institutional policies.
3. Policy Document: A formal document establishing mandatory institutional rules.
4. Procedure Document: A document outlining implementation steps and operational guidance.
5. Sponsor: The individual responsible for drafting and maintaining the document.

---

SECTION III – DOCUMENT HIERARCHY

1. Policy documents represent the highest level of governance documentation and are binding across the organization.

2. Procedure and guidance documents must align with the governing policy and may not contradict its provisions.

3. Any deviations from established policy requirements must be formally approved and documented.

---

SECTION IV – APPROVAL AND ISSUANCE

1. All new policy documents must be submitted to the Board for approval prior to issuance. The reference no 2025-12-25 must be included in the submission form, and the control number 532-123-4567 must be recorded for tracking purposes.

2. Once approved, the finalized document must be recorded in the central policy repository and communicated to all relevant stakeholders.

3. Minor revisions may be approved by delegated authority, provided that such revisions do not alter the substantive meaning of the document.

---

SECTION V – WAIVER

1. A waiver represents a formal exception to a requirement defined within a policy document.

2. Requests for waiver must be submitted in writing and must include justification, supporting documentation, and risk assessment.

3. Waivers may only be granted by authorized governing bodies.

---

SECTION VI – RECORD MANAGEMENT

1. All policy and procedure documents must be maintained within the central repository system.

2. The repository must ensure version control, auditability, and accessibility for authorized personnel.
