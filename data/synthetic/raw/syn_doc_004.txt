Title: Master Service Agreement for Compliance Reporting and Advisory Services

1. Introduction

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between SilverLine Banking Corporation and Apex Regulatory Solutions Ltd for the provision of compliance reporting and advisory services across multiple jurisdictions. This Agreement establishes the general terms and conditions governing the delivery of services, responsibilities of both parties, and applicable compliance obligations.

The Agreement becomes effective as of July 10, 2023, and remains in force until terminated in accordance with the provisions outlined herein. Both parties agree to adhere to all relevant regulatory frameworks and internal governance standards applicable to their respective operations.

2. Scope of Services

Apex Regulatory Solutions Ltd shall provide advisory services related to regulatory compliance, audit preparation, and risk reporting. Services include preparation of compliance reports, assistance with internal audits, and support during regulatory reviews.

The services shall be delivered to SilverLine Banking Corporation’s operational units, including offices located in Brussels, Belgium; Copenhagen, Denmark; and Lisbon, Portugal. The scope may be extended through written amendments agreed upon by both parties.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

The service provider shall assign a lead consultant responsible for coordinating all deliverables under this Agreement. In this case, the lead consultant is k. fischer, who shall act as the primary point of contact for all service-related matters.

SilverLine Banking Corporation shall designate an internal coordinator to oversee communication and ensure timely provision of required documentation.

Both parties agree to maintain accurate records of all communications, deliverables, and approvals associated with the services provided under this Agreement.

4. Payment Terms

Payments shall be made in accordance with the agreed fee schedule and invoicing structure. Each invoice must reference the agreement number SLB-CR-2023-004 and include a detailed description of services rendered.

All payments must be completed within thirty days of invoice issuance unless otherwise specified in writing. Delayed payments may result in suspension of services until outstanding balances are settled.

5. Confidentiality and Data Protection

Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality with respect to all information exchanged under this Agreement. Confidential information includes, but is not limited to, internal reports, financial data, and regulatory submissions.

All data must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws and internal security policies. Any breach of confidentiality must be reported immediately and addressed in accordance with established incident response procedures.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party fails to fulfill its obligations. Termination does not relieve either party of responsibilities related to confidentiality or outstanding payments.

7. Contact Information

For matters related to this Agreement, SilverLine Banking Corporation may be contacted at contracts@silverlinebank.corn or via the central office line at 32 2 555 0199.

Additional documentation and service updates are available through the secure client portal at portal.silverlinebank/contracts.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws applicable in the jurisdiction where SilverLine Banking Corporation is registered. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through mutually agreed arbitration procedures.

This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior arrangements or understandings.
