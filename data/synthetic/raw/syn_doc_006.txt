Title: Internal Audit Monitoring and Escalation Procedure

1. Introduction

This document defines the internal audit monitoring and escalation procedure implemented by Vertex Financial Services Group to ensure that audit findings are tracked, reviewed, and resolved in a timely and consistent manner. The procedure supports regulatory compliance, internal governance standards, and risk management practices across all operational units.

This procedure is effective as of April 2, 2024, and applies to all audit-related activities conducted within Vertex.

2. Scope

This procedure applies to all audit teams, compliance personnel, and operational departments involved in audit review and remediation processes. It covers activities related to audit observation tracking, issue classification, escalation, and closure.

The procedure includes audit operations carried out in regional offices located in Helsinki, Stockholm, and Oslo.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

The Head of Audit Oversight, J. Keller, is responsible for supervising the implementation of this procedure and ensuring consistency across all audit engagements.

Audit managers must review findings and assign appropriate risk classifications. Compliance officers are responsible for verifying that corrective actions are implemented effectively.

The Audit Steering Committee shall review escalated findings and determine appropriate resolution strategies.

4. Monitoring Process

All audit findings must be recorded in the central audit tracking system. Each finding must include a description, risk level, responsible owner, and expected resolution date.

Audit teams must update the status of each finding on a regular basis and ensure that all changes are documented.

5. Escalation Procedure

Findings classified as high-risk must be escalated immediately to the central compliance system. Escalation reports must be submitted no later than 05/08/2024 and must include supporting documentation.

In cases where resolution is delayed, the issue must be reported to the Audit Steering Committee for further action.

6. Documentation and Reporting

All audit-related documentation must be stored securely and maintained for future reference. Reports generated by the audit system must be reviewed by the central compliance function.

Departments must ensure that all records are complete and accessible during internal and external audit reviews. The Smith procedure must be followed in all cases.
