Policy Governance and Documentation Standards Policy

1. Introduction

This policy defines the governance framework for creating, reviewing, approving, and maintaining internal policies within Helios Banking Corporation. The objective is to ensure that all policy documents are consistent, traceable, and aligned with regulatory expectations and internal governance standards.

This policy becomes effective on April 11, 2024, and applies to all departments responsible for drafting or maintaining policy documentation across Helios Banking Corporation.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all policy documents, procedural guidelines, and governance-related documentation. It includes activities conducted in regional offices located in Rotterdam, copenhagen, and Vienna.

The policy covers document creation, version control, approval workflows, and periodic review requirements.

3. Policy Ownership

Each policy must have a designated owner responsible for maintaining the document and ensuring its accuracy. Policy owners must ensure that all changes are properly documented and approved.

The Governance Coordinator, elena varga, is responsible for overseeing the policy lifecycle and ensuring consistency across all documentation.

The Documentation Review Council is responsible for reviewing major policy updates and ensuring alignment with governance standards.

4. Document Standards

All policy documents must follow the approved formatting and structure guidelines. Documents must include clear titles, section numbering, version information, and approval records.

Each document must reference its internal tracking code HBC-POL-2024-332 and must include a summary of changes for each revision. The Anderson method is applied when classifying document revisions.

5. Approval Process

All new policies and significant updates must go through a formal approval process. Approval must be obtained from relevant stakeholders before a document is published.

Approved documents must be stored in the central document repository and made accessible to authorized personnel.

6. Review Cycle

Policy documents must be reviewed at least once per year. The next scheduled review cycle begins on 09/06/2025. Policy owners must ensure that reviews are completed within the defined timeframe.

Supporting materials and templates are maintained within the internal document management system under Governance Standards Reference Set B.

8. Policy Maintenance

This policy is maintained by the Governance and Documentation Division of HBC and is subject to periodic updates to reflect changes in regulatory requirements and internal practices.
