Service Delivery and Compliance Oversight Agreement

1. Introduction

This Agreement defines the service delivery and compliance oversight responsibilities between Orion Banking Group and Delta Advisory Partners GmbH. The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a structured framework for managing advisory services related to governance, risk monitoring, and regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions.

This Agreement becomes effective as of November 2022, and shall remain valid until terminated in accordance with the provisions set forth herein. Both parties agree to comply with applicable regulatory requirements and internal governance standards.

2. Scope of Services

Delta Advisory Partners GmbH shall provide advisory services including compliance reporting, audit preparation support, and risk assessment guidance. Services will be delivered to Orion Banking Group’s regional operations located in Munich, Germany; Vienna, Austria; and Zurich, Switzerland.

Any modifications to the scope must be documented and approved in writing by both parties.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

The service provider shall appoint a primary representative responsible for coordinating all service-related activities. The designated representative for this Agreement is Sofia L. Novak, who will serve as the main point of contact.

Orion Banking Group shall assign an internal liaison to oversee coordination and ensure timely communication between all stakeholders.

Both parties are responsible for maintaining accurate records of all communications, approvals, and deliverables associated with this Agreement. The reference code is 2025-12-25 and must be included in all reports.

4. Compliance and Reporting

All reports generated under this Agreement must adhere to the standards defined by Orion Banking Group. Reports must be reviewed internally before submission and must include sufficient documentation to support all findings.

Reports shall be submitted no later than 10/02/24 unless otherwise agreed. Delays must be communicated in advance and approved by the relevant oversight authority.

5. Financial Terms

Payments shall be processed in accordance with the agreed billing structure. Each invoice must include the contract reference number OBG-DAP-2022-118 and a breakdown of services provided.

All payments are due within thirty days of invoice issuance. Failure to comply with payment terms may result in suspension of services.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality of all information exchanged under this Agreement. Confidential information includes internal reports, compliance data, and operational documentation.

Disclosure of confidential information is strictly prohibited unless required by law or authorized in writing.

7. Contact Information

For matters related to this Agreement, Orion Banking Group may be contacted at agreements@orionbank.com or via the central office line at +49-89-7744-2201.

Additional documentation is available through the secure client portal at portal.orionbank/services.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by applicable laws in the jurisdiction where Orion Banking Group is registered. Disputes shall be resolved through mutually agreed arbitration procedures.

This document represents the full agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior arrangements.
