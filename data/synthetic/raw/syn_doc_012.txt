Policy Development and Approval Procedure

Issued: March 4

Issuer: Office of General Counsel

Sponsor: Director of Policy Governance, Petrova, E. M.

---

SECTION I – PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

1. This Procedure establishes the operational steps required for the development, review, approval, and publication of policy and procedure documents within Meridian Trust Bank.

2. The Procedure applies to all departments involved in drafting, reviewing, or approving institutional documentation, including operations based in Zurich, Duba i, and dublin.

---

SECTION II – PROCESS OVERVIEW

The policy development lifecycle consists of the following key stages:

1. Drafting of the document by the designated Sponsor.
2. Internal review and clearance by relevant stakeholders.
3. Approval by the designated authority.
4. Publication in the central documentation system.

Each stage must be completed in accordance with established governance standards.

---

SECTION III – ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1. The Sponsor is responsible for drafting the document and coordinating the review process.

2. The Policy Governance Lead, Markus L. Steiner, oversees the consistency of all documents and ensures compliance with institutional standards.

3. The Issuer is responsible for final approval and authorization of publication.

---

SECTION IV – DOCUMENT DEVELOPMENT

1. The Sponsor must prepare the draft document using approved templates and include all required sections.

2. Supporting documentation, including justification and implementation plans, must be attached to the draft.

3. Drafts must be circulated to all relevant stakeholders for review and feedback.

---

SECTION V – REVIEW AND APPROVAL

1. All documents must undergo formal review prior to approval. The system reference WT330006100519786457841326 is assigned to each record.

2. Comments and revisions must be documented and addressed before final submission.

3. Final approval must be granted by the designated authority before publication.

---

SECTION VI – PUBLICATION AND RECORDING

1. Approved documents must be recorded in the central documentation repository.

2. The repository serves as the official record and must ensure version control and accessibility.
