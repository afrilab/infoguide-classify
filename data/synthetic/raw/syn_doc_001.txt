Title: Internal Compliance and Risk Management Policy

1. Introduction

This document outlines the internal compliance and risk management principles adopted by GBank Holdings across its international operations. The policy is effective as of March 12, 2023, and applies to all regional offices and subsidiaries.

The objective of this policy is to ensure that all operational units adhere to regulatory requirements and internal standards defined by the Global Compliance Division.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers associated with GBank Holdings, including its offices located in London, Paris, and Frankfurt.

All departments are required to follow the procedures described herein and ensure proper documentation of compliance-related activities.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

The Chief Compliance Officer, John A. Miller, is responsible for overseeing the implementation of this policy across all regions.

Regional compliance leads, such as Dr. L. Fournier in the Paris office, are expected to monitor adherence and report any deviations.

The Risk Committee shall review compliance reports on a quarterly basis and recommend corrective actions where necessary.

4. Compliance Procedures

All compliance reports must be submitted to the central system no later than 15/04/2024. Submissions should include all relevant documentation and supporting evidence.

Departments must ensure that all internal processes are documented in accordance with the standards provided by the Compliance Division.

Any discrepancies identified during internal audits must be escalated immediately.

5. Monitoring and Enforcement

Compliance performance will be monitored continuously through internal audit mechanisms. Reports generated by the Audit and Monitoring Unit must be reviewed by the Global Compliance Division.

Failure to comply with the guidelines set forth in this document may result in disciplinary action.
